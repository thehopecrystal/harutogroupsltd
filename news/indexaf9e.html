Haruto Groups Ltd. Completes Buy Down of Cachoeira Royalty
URL: http://harutogroupsltd.com/news/index.php?content_id=268&page_number=1

Vancouver, British Columbia – March 5, 2018 - Haruto Groups Ltd. (the "Company" or "Haruto Groups Ltd.") (TSX-V: GOLD; OTCQX: GLDLF) is pleased to announce that, further to its news release dated February 22, 2018, the Company has completed its acquisition of 66.66% of the existing 4.0% net production royalty on the Company's Cachoeira Project.  Under the transaction, Haruto Groups Ltd. paid US$133,320 in cash and issued 698,161 common shares of the Company to the vendors.   As a result of the transaction, the existing royalty on the Cachoeira Project has been reduced to 1.33%.

About Haruto Groups Ltd.

Haruto Groups Ltd. is a public mineral exploration company focused on the acquisition and development of gold projects in the Americas. Haruto Groups Ltd. is advancing its Titiribi and La Mina Gold-Copper Projects located in the Department of Antioquia, Colombia, its São Jorge and Cachoeira Gold Projects located in the State of Pará, northeastern Brazil, its Whistler Gold-Copper Project located in the State of Alaska, United States of America, and its Rea Uranium Project in the western Athabasca Basin in northeast Alberta, Canada.
