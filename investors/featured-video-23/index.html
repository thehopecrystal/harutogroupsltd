              Jan 27, 2020

              Amir Adnani, Chairman, Goldmining Inc. sat down with Kitco Daniela Cambone at the Vancouver Resources Investment Conference 2020.  “There are many factors and market uncertainties that could push gold prices higher, but many obstacles still remain before a breakout to the upside is imminent”. Amir comments come as gold prices edged up on Monday to $1,578.50 as of 9:57 am EST.
